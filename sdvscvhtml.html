Technical Projects

2025–
cicadas2 — reference implementation supporting the CICADAS-II BOOGALOO manuscript and the Stanford research program. Lead developer; repository private pending publication with the manuscript.
  • End-to-end simulation framework for non-convulsive seizures in the ICU: synthetic patient, latent disease dynamics, pharmacokinetics/pharmacodynamics, sparse Poisson seizure observations, real-time state estimation, treatment strategies, and a daily-hazard mortality model.
  • Implements an extended Kalman filter on the log-rate state, one-compartment PK with infusion and bolus dosing, Hill and linear PD suppression, five treatment strategies including adaptive variants, and parametric g-formula recovery of causal effects from confounded observational data.
  • Two complete implementations — MATLAB reference and a verified Python port (NumPy/SciPy) — with a pytest/MATLAB cross-fixture suite that locks numerical equivalence.
  • Includes the LaTeX manuscript draft, a reproducibility harness that regenerates every reported number, and a figure pipeline in both languages.
2023–
uni / acad-cli — academic workflow automation platform. Lead developer. github.com/sdvstephens/uni
  • Terminal-based academic workflow as a single Python CLI (acad, wrapped by a fish-shell front end) consolidating course management, lecture and problem-set scaffolding, grade and GPA tracking, and a live status dashboard; in daily use across coursework and tutoring.
  • Generates lecture and problem-set documents in Org-mode and LaTeX, with master-file assembly, latexmk builds against a shared preamble, and an Ipe figure-insertion pipeline driven from Neovim/VimTeX.
  • Integrates Taskwarrior and Timewarrior for deadline-aware, time-tracked course management; stores metadata, grades, and notes as human-readable JSON under Git.
  • Released as free software under the MIT license — pipx install acad-cli.
2025–
rmpp-stabilizer — real-time stroke stabilization for the reMarkable Paper Pro. Sole developer; repository private pending release.
  • C++ LD_PRELOAD shared library (MIT) that intercepts evdev pen input before the device's note-taking application, smoothing handwriting in real time to reduce jitter in mathematical notation and diagrams.
  • Configurable smoothing: moving average, Gaussian-weighted, string-pull, and the One Euro filter (Casiez et al., 2012); clean-room reimplementation informed by Krita's open-source stabilizer (GPL-2.0).
  • Docker-based aarch64 toolchain for reproducible builds against the device's embedded Linux environment.
2025–
rmpp-wikipedia — offline Wikipedia client for the reMarkable Paper Pro. Sole developer; repository private pending release.
  • Qt/QML reading interface communicating over loopback HTTP with a small native zimserver backend (aarch64, built on Kiwix kiwix-tools and libzim), so a full .zim snapshot on USB or SD can be browsed entirely offline.
  • Designed for low-power E Ink reading; coexists with the stock reader via a Qt resource-rebuilder integration.

Teaching and Curriculum Development

An open, self-paced mathematics curriculum organized around mathematical structure rather than procedure. Companion worksheets, problem sets, and lecture notes are published at samuel-stephens.com; video lectures are scripted in LaTeX before recording and released openly under the YouTube handle @Vichaama. Recording and upload are pending; the curriculum is the current deliverable.

  • Basic Mathematics (ongoing) — structural treatment of arithmetic, algebra, and elementary number theory in three parts: Arithmetic as Structure, Algebraic Foundations, and Number Theory and Patterns.
  • Integrated Mathematics I (planned) — algebra (linear systems, polynomials, quadratics, rationals, radicals, complex numbers), Euclidean and coordinate geometry with transformations as group actions, three-dimensional geometry, and a number-theory strand from divisibility through modular arithmetic and the Chinese remainder theorem.
  • Integrated Mathematics II (planned) — single-variable calculus from limits through series, an introductory linear algebra strand, and a first formal pass through abstract algebra, using structure to unify the three strands.
  • Integrated Mathematics III (planned) — proof-based real analysis (construction of the reals, sequences and series, continuity and differentiation, Riemann integration with a glimpse of Lebesgue), abstract algebra (rings, fields, Galois theory), and onward to complex analysis and point-set topology.
