Technical Projects

Research and Academic Software
2025–
cicadas2 — Reference implementation supporting the CICADAS-II manuscript and the Stanford research program. Primary developer. Repository currently private; will be published with the manuscript.
  • Two complete implementations: MATLAB reference (the original) and a verified Python port (NumPy/SciPy), each with a full pytest/MATLAB cross-fixture test suite that locks numerical equivalence
  • Implements an Extended Kalman Filter on the log-rate state, one-compartment PK with infusion and bolus dosing, Hill/linear PD suppression, five treatment strategies (including adaptive variants), and parametric g-formula recovery of causal treatment effects from confounded observational data
  • Includes the LaTeX manuscript draft, a reproducibility harness that regenerates every reported number, and a figure-generation pipeline in both MATLAB and Python
2023–
uni — Command-line tool for managing coursework, lecture notes, and problem sets in LaTeX and Org-mode. Sole developer; built for personal use and released openly for others. github.com/sdvstephens/uni
  • Built an integrated, terminal-based academic workflow as a single Python CLI (acad, exposed through a uni fish-shell wrapper) consolidating course management, lecture and problem-set scaffolding, grade and GPA tracking, and a live status dashboard, in daily personal use across coursework, audits, and tutoring
  • Generates lecture and problem-set documents in both Org-mode and LaTeX from the command line, with master-file assembly, plain pdflatex via latexmk against a shared preamble, and an Ipe figure-insertion pipeline driven from first-class neovim/vimtex integration
  • Integrated with Taskwarrior and Timewarrior for deadline-aware, time-tracked course management surfaced through the dashboard
  • Stores course metadata, grades, and notes as human-readable JSON under Git version control
  • Published as a FOSS project under MIT—pipx install acad-cli; sdvstephens/acad-cli—inspired by the late Gilles Castel

Independent Curriculum

Open mathematics curriculum for self-paced learners, starting from arithmetic. It takes a structural approach to mathematics, shaped by the shortcomings of the 1960s New Math, and a Humboldtian, dialectical view of education. Worksheets, problem sets, and lecture notes are published at samuel-stephens.com/notes/integrated-maths; lectures are scripted in LaTeX and will be recorded for YouTube (@Vichaama).

  • Basic Mathematics (in progress)—pre-algebra, arithmetic as structure, algebraic foundations, number theory, geometric reasoning, counting and probability, and functions, laid out lecture by lecture
  • Integrated Mathematics I (notes online)—sets and proof; equations, functions, and graphs; quadratics; polynomials; sequences, series, and induction; with graph theory, combinatorics, and probability planned
  • Integrated Mathematics II (notes online)—trigonometry; parametric equations, polar coordinates, and complex numbers; the real numbers; groups, rings, and fields; with vector spaces and topology planned
  • Integrated Mathematics III and IV (planned)—advanced linear algebra, advanced group theory, real analysis, and topology; then complex analysis, differential equations, Lie groups, geometry, and differential geometry
