## Ribosomes and the rough ER

- **Ribosomes:** structures in the cytoplasm that translate mRNA to manufacture proteins from raw materials in the form of **amino acids**.
- **Rough endoplasmic reticulum (rough ER):** what many ribosomes are attached to. More abundant in neurons than in most other non-neural cells. These are also the Nissl bodies (the things that got dyed!).
- Ribosomes that are not attached to the rough ER are called **free ribosomes** (as they float freely through the cytoplasm).
- **Polyribosomes:** free ribosomes attached together by a thread; the thread is a single strand of mRNA. The ribosomes on any one strand are working together to create many copies of the same protein.

If the protein is to be inserted into the membrane of a cell or organelle, then the mRNA goes to the rough ER; otherwise it stays free-floating in the cytosol. The rough ER traps the proteins to send them to the Golgi apparatus.

Special membrane proteins (assembled in the neuron’s rough ER) are what provide our neurons with information-processing abilities.

## Smooth ER and the Golgi apparatus

**Smooth ER:** heterogeneous (not all the same); it does a bunch of different things and can even be extensions of the rough ER.

**Golgi apparatus:** sorts proteins post-assembly for chemical processing and delivery (such as to the axons and dendrites).

## Mitochondria

**Mitochondrion/mitochondria:** 1 µm long. Consists of folds of an inner membrane called **cristae** (singular **crista**). Between the cristae is the **matrix**. The mitos provide “**cellular respiration**,” a process where they take in **pyruvic acid** (from the sugars, proteins, and fats that you digest) and oxygen floating in the cytosol.

Inside the mito, the pyruvic acid begins the **Krebs cycle**, which then provides energy for reactions in the cristae (the **electron-transport chain**) that add phosphate to **adenosine diphosphate** (ADP), creating **adenosine triphosphate (ATP)**. ATP is the energy source of the cell. The mito then “exhales” 17 ATP molecules for every molecule of pyruvic acid taken in. ATP is VERY important and powers most neural activity.

## The cytoskeleton

**Cytoskeleton:** holds the structure of the cell; the cell membrane is kind of draped over the cytoskeleton.
