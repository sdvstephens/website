The notes come in two parts.

**Basic Neuroscience** (chapters 1–5) follows PSY 212. Each numbered lecture is written up from the course's lecture slides, with its review questions gathered at the end. My own notebook from the reading sits with the first chapter.

**Geometry of Neuroscience** (chapters 6–12) follows Matilde Marcolli's course [Ma 191b, *Geometry of Neuroscience*](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~matilde/Ma191Winter2026.html) at Caltech, winter 2026. Each section is written up from her slides, in the order of her schedule: structures in the brain, then vision, then language. The introduction also has the opening lecture of the 2017 course, which she taught with Doris Tsao.
