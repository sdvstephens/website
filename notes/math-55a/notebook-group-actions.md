:::definition
An **action** of a group $G$ on a set $S$ is a homomorphism
$$G\longrightarrow\operatorname{Perm}S=\{\text{bijections }S\to S\}.$$
That is, we have a map
$$G\times S\longrightarrow S,\qquad (g,s)\longmapsto g\cdot s.$$
:::
