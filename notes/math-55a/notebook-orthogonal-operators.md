Let $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : V \times V \to \RR$ be an inner product. We want maps that preserve lengths and angles.

:::definition Orthogonal operator
$T : V \to V$ is an **orthogonal operator** if for all $u, v \in V$,
$$
\langle Tu, Tv \rangle = \langle u, v \rangle.
$$
$\iff T$ preserves lengths and angles $\equiv T$ is an **isometry**.
:::
