> FUN FACTS: 1777: Euler calls $\sqrt{-1} = i$.

$\FF$ is our field and is either $\RR$ or $\CC$. A number in the field $\equiv$ **scalar**.

:::definition List and length
- A **list** is an ordered collection of elements.
- Its **length** is the number $n$ of elements in it.

A list of length $n$ is called an **$n$-tuple**.
:::

$\FF^n$ is the set of all lists of length $n$ of elements of $\FF$.

**Addition** $+$ on $V$ as a vector space assigns an element $u + v \in V$ to each pair of elements $u, v \in V$.

**Scalar multiplication** (SCALAR MULT OVER VECTOR) assigns an element $\lambda v \in V$ to each $\lambda \in \FF$ and each $v \in V$:
$$\forall v \in V,\ \forall \lambda \in \FF: \quad \lambda v \in V.$$
