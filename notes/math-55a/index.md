Each numbered lecture is transcribed from Professor Denis Auroux’s handwritten lecture notes for the course, with the date it was given. His notes for a lecture often ran past what he got through, and the next lecture picked up from there; those leftover pages are folded at the end of a lecture, with a link to the lecture that covered them. There are no notes for lectures 13 and 27. His two midterm review sheets and two review sheets for the final sit with the lectures. Each of these pages links the PDF of his notes it was transcribed from; for now the lecture PDFs are behind a password, and the review sheets are open.

After the lectures, each chapter has my own notes on the same material: first the sections of my book on it, *Studies in Algebra and Group Theory* ([PDF](/notes/math-55a/studies-in-algebra-and-group-theory.pdf)), then the notebooks I kept through the term. The last chapter is from the book alone.
