WTS:
$$\prod_pG_p\cong G.$$

1. Construct $\varphi:\prod_pG_p\to G$ with restrictions given by the inclusions $G_p\hookrightarrow G$.

2. For each prime $p\in\PP$, write $|G|=p^km$ with $p\nmid m$, and let $r$ be such that $m\mid r$ and $r\equiv1\pmod{p^k}$.
3. Consider $\phi:G\to G$, $g\mapsto g^r$ (a homomorphism).
4. Show that $\operatorname{Im}(\phi)=G_p$.
5. Construct $\varphi^{-1}$ using $\operatorname{Im}(\phi)=G_p$.
