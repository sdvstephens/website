Let $g, h \in G$. When $G$ acts upon itself via conjugation, $g$ acts on $h$ by $h \mapsto ghg^{-1}$. This is a group action, and it is also a group homomorphism
$$
G \to \Aut(G) \subseteq \operatorname{Perm}(G).
$$
