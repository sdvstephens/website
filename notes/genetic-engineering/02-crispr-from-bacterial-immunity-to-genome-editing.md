CRISPR with Rodolphe Barrangou. CRISPR “is a genetic iPhone” because of the spacers, the seemingly random sequences between the repeats.

**CRISPR**: clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic repeats. The repeats sit between numbered boxes, the spacers, which are unique sequences; the *cas* genes sit next to the array.

![A CRISPR locus: cas9, cas1, cas6 and cas7 genes, a leader, and an array of repeats alternating with numbered spacers](/notes/genetic-engineering/figures/d1-crispr-locus.svg)

## Genetic hypervariability and phenotypic correlations

The strains showed genetic and phenotypic similarity (correlation).

- DGCC7710: DuPont (??), the yoghurt maker. 4 *cas* systems, 3 subtypes.

- **1985**: isolated.
- **1989**: bacteriophage Φ858. About $10^{31}$ phages exist; Φ858 kills 99% of the bacteria.
- **1990**: a **bacteriophage-insensitive mutant**, DGCC7778, an isolated resistant variant from forced evolution.
- **2005**: DNA sequencing and comparison of the CRISPR loci in DGCC7778 vs. DGCC7710.

Exopolysaccharides determine yoghurt structure (sticky and stiff, or creamy and drizzly).
