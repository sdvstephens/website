We know there is no largest integer, because when we add $1$ to an integer $k$ we get $k + 1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A(1)&: \ 0 < \tfrac{1^3}{3} \\
A(2)&: \ 1^2 < \tfrac{2^3}{3} \\
A(3)&: \ 1^2 + 2^2 < \tfrac{3^3}{3}
\end{aligned}
\qquad\qquad
A(n): \ 1^2 + 2^2 + \dots + (n-1)^2 < \frac{n^3}{3}
$$
