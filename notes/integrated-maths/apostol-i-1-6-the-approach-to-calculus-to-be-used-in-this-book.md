We will let the real numbers ($\RR$) be axioms, and also some of their properties. (TF does this mean??)

:::remark Correction
The real numbers are not themselves an axiom. Apostol takes the real numbers as undefined objects, as in a deductive system, and takes some of their properties as axioms.
:::

What is the “least-upper-bound” axiom / completeness / continuity axiom? It literally just means there are no gaps in $\RR$’s number line.
