The sequence I planned for myself, from arithmetic up:

- **Basic Mathematics:** pre-algebra, arithmetic, Vedic mathematics, number theory, geometry, probability and counting, functions and sets, axioms and the Peano axioms.
- **Integrated Maths I:** intermediate algebra (the Art of Problem Solving books), graph theory, combinatorics, probability and counting, proofs and trigonometry.
- **Integrated Maths II:** precalculus, calculus, groups, rings and fields, vector spaces, topology.
- **Integrated Maths III:** advanced linear algebra, advanced group theory, real analysis, topology.
- **Integrated Maths IV:** complex analysis, differential equations, Lie groups, geometry, differential geometry.

Basic Mathematics is laid out lecture by lecture below; those pages are still to be written. The later parts collect my notebooks where they belong. Integrated Maths I has my College Algebra notes, Art of Problem Solving’s *Intermediate Algebra*, and Jay Cummings’s *Proofs*. Integrated Maths II has AoPS *Precalculus*, Apostol’s *Calculus*, and Birkhoff and Mac Lane’s *A Survey of Modern Algebra*. Some of Integrated Maths III and IV is already elsewhere on the site, in [Math 55a](/notes/math-55a/), [Math 55b](/notes/math-55b/) and [Measure Theory](/notes/measure-theory/).

Where one of my notebook answers or proofs is wrong, a short correction sits beside it.
