## Real and Imaginary Parts

:::problem{6.22}
(a) Let $r = |z|$. Express $z$ in terms of $r$.

:::solution
Write $z = a + bi$, so $\overline{z} = a - bi$ and
$$|z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = r, \qquad r^2 = a^2 + b^2.$$
Also $\overline{z}z = a^2 + b^2$, so
$$z\overline{z} = |z|^2 = r^2 \implies z = \frac{r^2}{\overline{z}},$$
but that still has $z$ in it (as $\overline{z}$).
:::
:::
