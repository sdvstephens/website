# 06-16-2026


Some new feelings I have encountered—said feelings are old and known, but surprising and welcome, for every time it happens it harbors old novelty—I would like to note here: 
A maths problem you have worked on for well over an hour, possibly several, seems entirely perplexing; you are sure you have missed something as your path feels improperly hardscrabbled. And then it dawns on you: aha! it is so incredibly simple, stupidly simple in fact! A short 1-2 page answer (or as small as 1-2 lines) more than suffices. You feel goofy for the time lost, relieved you were able to solve it, and satisfied at the elegance of the solution. 
A maths problem you have been excited to work on: you saw it out of the corner of your eye while scrolling through the pset and excitedly finished all the other problems before in order to get to this one (order depends on the pset, however; most of the time I jump around on problems, in this particular case however, I went in order), only to, upon tackling the problem, solve it immediately! Maybe there is a bit of joy with it, but it is quite bittersweet. You were hoping to tango with the problem for a bit, allowing yourself to know it. Ah well.
