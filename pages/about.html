Born in rural Texas, Samuel Desiderius Vichaama Stephens is a student and researcher working in neuroscience and mathematics. He holds, concurrently, an internship at Mass General Brigham (MGB/MGH) and a research appointment at the Stanford University School of Medicine (Department of Neurology); formerly held a research appointment at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School. His employment work centers on statistical and simulation methods for critical-care medicine: causal inference, pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic (PKPD) modeling, and survival optimization for seizure prediction and treatment. Outside of research, he has interests in the philosophy of natural science and of education, and in areas of pure mathematics such as algebraic geometry, number theory, and polyadic semigroups (despite being bummed by learning of Post's theorem). He is an aspiring academic, hoping to one day be a professor of either mathematics or genetics working in mathematical modeling of cognition and mental phenomena along with the potential genetic applications downstream from said modeling (a vague gesture in the realm of mathematical theoretical neuroscience). He hopes to borrow many of the sophisticated modeling techniques from mathematical physics and pure mathematics rather than a strictly statistical approach. Stephens is also an avid reader and writer, accruing a great number of essays, some of which appear on his blog. Stephens writes works of poetry and political philosophy in his leisure time. He is heavily inspired by Baudelaire, Rimbaud, Shelley, Yeats, Beckett, Oliver, and most notably, Proust.

Technical Projects

uni — Command-Line Tool for Coursework and LaTeX Notes

Sole developer of an integrated, terminal-based academic workflow built as a single Python CLI (acad, exposed through a uni fish-shell wrapper; GitHub), consolidating course management, lecture and problem-set scaffolding, grade and GPA tracking, and a live status dashboard. In daily personal use across coursework, audits, and tutoring.

  • Generates lecture and problem-set documents in both Org-mode and LaTeX from the command line, with master-file assembly, pdflatex via latexmk against a shared preamble, and an Ipe figure-insertion pipeline driven from first-class Neovim/VimTeX integration
  • Integrated with Taskwarrior and Timewarrior for deadline-aware, time-tracked course management surfaced through the dashboard
  • Stores course metadata, grades, and notes as human-readable JSON under Git version control

Built for personal use and published openly for others as a FOSS project under MIT (pipx install acad-cli), inspired by the late Gilles Castel.

cicadas2 — CICADAS-II BOOGALOO Reference Implementation

Primary developer of the end-to-end simulation framework, written in MATLAB and Python, grounding the CICADAS-II BOOGALOO manuscript and the Stanford research program. Models the full ICU non-convulsive-seizure pipeline — synthetic patient, latent disease dynamics, PK/PD, sparse Poisson seizure observations, real-time state estimation, treatment strategies, and a daily-hazard mortality model. Implemented in both MATLAB (reference) and a verified Python port (NumPy/SciPy) with a cross-fixture test suite locking numerical equivalence; includes an Extended Kalman Filter, one-compartment PK, Hill/linear PD suppression, five treatment strategies, and parametric g-formula recovery of causal effects. Repository private; to be published with the manuscript (potentially).

graphite — Graphing & 3D Calculator for reMarkable Paper Pro

Sole developer of a from-scratch graphing and 3D calculator for the reMarkable Paper Pro that renders 2D, parametric, polar, and implicit curves along with shaded, gradient-colored 3D surfaces, and inserts them into the active notebook or document as native, resizable objects. A QML (Qt Quick Canvas) frontend drives the UI and rendering; a Python backend (sympy/numpy/scipy) over a Unix socket handles parsing, symbolic computation, sampling, 3D meshes, and special functions; the app ships to the device via XOVI/AppLoad. The expression engine is developed and Node-tested standalone before touching any UI. Currently at the M1.1 prototype stage — a working 2D core — with the headline document-insertion feature and 3D surfaces planned for later milestones. Repository private; in active development.

BetterCanvas (now known as Better Campus) — Safari Web Extension for Canvas LMS

Sole developer of a native Safari Web Extension (Xcode/Swift host app plus a Safari extension target) that improves the day-to-day Canvas LMS reading and submission workflow on macOS and iOS/iPadOS. Repository private; to be published once stable.

Defunct / Discontinued Projects

rmpp-stabilizer — Handwriting Stabilization for reMarkable Paper Pro

Sole developer of an LD_PRELOAD shared library (C++, MIT) that intercepts evdev pen input before xochitl to smooth handwriting in real time, reducing jitter in mathematical notation and diagrams. Configurable smoothing algorithms (moving-average, Gaussian-weighted, Krita-style string pull, and the One Euro filter), built via a Docker-based aarch64 toolchain for reproducible builds. Discontinued.

rmpp-wikipedia — Offline Wikipedia Client for reMarkable Paper Pro

Sole developer of a Qt/QML reading UI that talks over loopback HTTP to a small native zimserver backend (aarch64, built on Kiwix kiwix-tools and libzim), so a full .zim Wikipedia snapshot on USB/SD can be browsed entirely offline. Designed for low-power E Ink reading alongside xochitl. Discontinued.
